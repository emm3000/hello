# App name options

Research for [issue #38](https://github.com/emm3000/hello/issues/38), checked on 2026-10-05. The owner chooses the name; this document does not rename the launcher, package or Play listing.

## Recommendation

Shortlist **Palvero**, with **Guardavo** as the alternative. Palvero is short, suggests personal words through “palabra” and returning to them through “volver”, and avoids tying the product to interviews or a single profession. This association is a proposed brand story, not an existing dictionary meaning. Its pronunciation is straightforward in Spanish (“pal-VE-ro”); English pronunciation should be tested with speakers before selection. Guardavo communicates saving more directly in Spanish but has a longer label and substantial Italian dictionary/search noise.

Choose a name-independent card mark from [the launcher concepts](design/launcher-mark/README.md) while deciding. My proposed pair is **Palvero + Card A**, subject to the owner's choice and a local trademark check. Keep Hello if avoiding a rename is the priority; choose it with the existing education-name overlap understood.

## Search method and limits

Google Play was checked through indexed official listing pages and direct app searches for the two shortlisted names with `gl=PE`, `hl=en_US`, `c=apps`. General searches used quoted candidate names, with `site:play.google.com/store/apps` to focus on Play listings where needed; unrelated books, films and review text were excluded from app-name findings. Play search is approximate and regional. “No exact-name app found” describes the inspected results, not reserved availability or an exhaustive similarity search.

The [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) interface was queried with each candidate in its **Wordmark** mode, with live and dead statuses included and no class restriction. The table records the observed counts or “No results found”. Wordmark mode can include containing phrases and related terms. These are US registry observations, not worldwide clearance, a legal opinion, or an assertion that an unregistered commercial name is available. The two live Lexora results were observed as screening evidence; their relevance to education/software was not established.

[WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/) was attempted, but the browser blocked access and the text browser could not expose searchable results. No WIPO result is claimed. The historical TESS system was not used; USPTO's current Trademark Search was accessible.

The local [INDECOPI Busca tu marca](https://pi.indecopi.gob.pe/buscatumarca/#/inicio) registry was also queried for **Palvero** and **Guardavo**, with both Vigente and No Vigente enabled and classes **9, 41 and 42** selected. Each broad phonetic search reported 200 results; the first displayed page included PALMER / LA VERO for Palvero and GUARDIAN for Guardavo. Using the visible **Búsqueda precisa** control returned **0 results** for each name. These exact-name observations do not clear the 200 similar-name results: their goods/services, current legal status and likelihood of confusion were not comprehensively evaluated. Before adopting either name, complete that similarity assessment and the international screen. No domain, GitHub Pages identity, mark registration or store-name reservation was checked or acquired in this research.
